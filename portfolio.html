45% Saved Case Study 1: How I Slashed Operational Costs by 45%

▶ Watch on YouTube

📉 The Challenge: Rising Procurement Costs & Inefficient Spending

The client was facing a financial crisis due to unchecked procurement expenses. Reliance on credit-based purchasing led to inflated prices, high interest, and a lack of transparency. There was no formal system to verify if the company was overpaying or underpaying vendors, resulting in significant profit leakage and high operational costs.

💰 Smart Cash Negotiations
  • Achieved a 45% reduction in costs by shifting from credit to cash-based procurement.
  • Negotiated "rock-bottom" prices by eliminating high interest premiums.
📑 The Three-Quote Rule
  • Required 03 quotations for every purchase to ensure no overpayment.
  • Brought in new vendors to create competition and drive prices down.
🔍 Market Price Checks
  • Performed spot-checks to ensure vendors stayed honest with pricing.
  • Kept strict oversight on every purchase for total transparency.
🤝 Better Credit Terms
  • Where credit was necessary, negotiated prices as close to cash rates as possible.
💰 Total Impact Created: 45% Reduction in Operational Costs

Before Audit Implementation Payment Errors Lack of Document Linkage

⚙️ The 7-Step Document Flow Mechanism for completion of documents and payment accuracy

I implemented a sequential verification process where each document feeds data into the next document, and all documents are linked with Purchase order, preventing manual errors:

  1. Purchase Order (PO): Sets the initial main document as a contract (i.e. Item + Rate).
  2. Delivery Challan: Vendor submits Delivery Challan after delivery.
  3. GRN: System auto-collects Item Name & Quantity from PO.
  4. Sale Invoice: Vendor submits; System auto-picks Rate from PO.
  5. Purchase Invoice: GRN is updated for invoice number because quantity and rate is already linked from PO, Invoice Posted to Party Ledger.
  6. Exemption/WHT: Tax deducted as per law.
  7. Payment Voucher: Final payment after 100% verification.
✅ Achievements: Payment Accuracy Ensured | Zero Double Payments | Complete Audit Trail
